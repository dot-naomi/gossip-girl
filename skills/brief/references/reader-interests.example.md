# Reader interests

What this reader keeps leaning into. Read before gathering material; weight known-interest items to the top of the briefing and run at least one search aimed at these keywords. Update from clear signals only: in-run reactions (follow-ups, "more of this", click-throughs) and, when the reader asks, ingested signals from other channels (forwards, replies, saves, reactions). Do not invent interests the reader never showed.

Format: `keyword / theme — tally — source — last seen (YYYY-MM-DD)`. **One row per theme.** This is a current-state tally, not an append-only log: when a theme comes up again, edit its existing row (increment the tally, update the date) rather than adding a second row for it. More tallies means surface it higher. Source is how the interest was earned: `in-run`, `forward`, `reply`, `saved`, `reaction`, or `pasted`. Stronger sources (forward > saved > reaction) can outweigh a higher tally of weak ones. Let stale, low-tally interests decay.
